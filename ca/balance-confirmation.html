User Documentation

Follow this checklist before sending balance confirmations in bulk.

1. Preparation

Download the Excel template and fill the Party List sheet from row 3. Ensure Email ID and Outstanding Balance are complete before upload.

2. Email Engines

Cloud mode works with Gmail or Outlook 365 using app passwords. Local mode connects with desktop Outlook through the offline launcher.

3. Templates

Use placeholders like {{party_name}} and {{amount}} in subject and body for personalized messages at scale.

4. Privacy and Safety

For sensitive audits, use the offline launcher to keep processing on your local environment.
