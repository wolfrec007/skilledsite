Welcome to SkilledCA! I created this platform to bridge the gap between two distinct yet increasingly converging worlds: Finance and Technology.

By profession, I am a CA Final Student currently doing my articleship at PKF Sridhar & Santhanam. I understand the intricacies of audits, taxation, compliance, and the daily challenges finance professionals face. But unlike the traditional path, I found my passion in automation and coding.

Why SkilledCA?

I realized that many CAs and finance professionals spend hours on repetitive tasks that could be solved with simple scripts or better tools. On the flip side, many developers don't understand the specific needs of the finance domain.

SkilledCA is my attempt to maintain a repository of my learnings and share them with the community. Whether you're a developer looking to understand fintech workflows or a CA wanting to learn Python to automate your Excel sheets, you're in the right place.
